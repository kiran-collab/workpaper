Miraflores Workpaper: system architecture

Everything runs in the browser as static files. The packet is never changed. Every human action is an event appended to a journal, and each screen is redrawn from fold(packet, journal) plus the chosen date and role.

What happens when the reviewer clicks Approve

  1. Daniel (reviewer) clicks Approve on the RSU basis adjustment.
  2. A.approve checks can(reviewer, approve), blocks self-approval, and notes it still needs the partner (over $1,000).
  3. journal.append stores an adjust.approve event with seq, actor and the season date.
  4. The event is saved to localStorage and posted on BroadcastChannel to any other open tab.
  5. The subscriber fires render(), here and in the other tabs.
  6. buildContext refolds every event over the packet and reprices the adjustment at the version's tax rates.
  7. Every view redraws. Sign-offs whose fingerprint covered the changed lines now show as reopened.

Why this shape

The packet stays true. The pipeline output is never edited, so the engine's number and the workpaper's number always sit side by side.

History is the database. Because state is a fold of events, the season clock can replay any date and undo is just another event.

Moving to a server is one swap. Replace localStorage and BroadcastChannel with a single append-and-subscribe API. The fold, checks and views stay as they are.
